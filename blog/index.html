San Francisco Real Estate

Insights & Market Updates

Neighborhood guides, buying and selling tips, and Caitlin's take on what's happening in the San Francisco market.

San Francisco is unlike any other real estate market in the world. It is a city of microclimates, micro markets, and micro pricing — where the value of a property can shift dramatically from one street to the next, and where understanding the nuances of each neighborhood is essential to making a sound buying or selling decision. Caitlin D'Aprano has spent over 14 years living and working in San Francisco, and she writes from genuine experience rather than from a script. Whether you are relocating from another city, buying your first home, selling a property you have owned for years, or simply trying to make sense of the market, you will find straightforward, honest, and useful information here. Reach out to Caitlin if you have any questions.
