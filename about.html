Meet Caitlin

Caitlin attended world-renowned university, The University of Melbourne, ranked 13 alongside institutions such as Harvard and Stanford. A BSc graduate with a major in psychology lends itself to the people aspect of real estate. Australia is known for its friendly, often funny and charming community and Caitlin carries these Australian philosophies into her everyday life in real estate.

Caitlin is a fourth-generation entrepreneur and third generation real estate professional. Caitlin founded her own businesses across various industries in both London and the US. This level of expertise comes with a breadth of experience related to real estate; finance, marketing, sales, design, customer service, management, negotiation and working with vendors. Fun Fact: Caitlin's luxury shoe brand made it into the top 100 businesses for a show featured on major UK television network the BBC.

Caitlin has been living in San Francisco for 14+ years but has also lived in London, Rome and Florence. Caitlin's diverse personal, geographic, and business background makes her the perfect middle person during a transaction. Caitlin thinks outside of the box, thrives in negotiations and zealously advocates for her clients to get them the best terms possible.

Caitlin is preferred by clients who value active listening, empathy and careful attention to their needs. Her approach and style caters to those who want someone intuitive, who is a bold advocate for their interests, who also understands the importance of sharp analysis and direct communication about strengths and weaknesses of each purchase or sale.

Caitlin is the ultimate San Francisco luxury real estate agent. Both buyers and sellers are thrilled to work with Caitlin because no detail goes missed. Give Caitlin a call today, at a minimum, to hear her endearing Australian accent.
